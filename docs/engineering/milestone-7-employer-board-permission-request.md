**Subject:** Permission to use a short job-posting excerpt as educational evidence in Miva Buddy

Hello [Company / Recruiting team],

We are building Miva Buddy, a learning companion for university Computer Science and Software Engineering students. We are evaluating [Company]'s public [Greenhouse / Ashby / Lever] job board as a source for a small contextual evidence card inside a course-grounded Learn conversation. Miva Buddy is not a job-search site, job directory, application service, or recruiting lead-generation product.

When a student's course material directly mentions a concrete technology and one of your currently published roles contains that same term, we would like to display only the role title, company, public location if supplied, a short verbatim sentence that directly names the technology, and a link to your original posting. We would identify [Company] and the source board. We would not reproduce full descriptions, collect applications, or send student information to your board.

Would [Company] authorize Miva Buddy to retrieve the public listing through [documented public API/feed name] and to display those limited fields and the bounded excerpt for this educational purpose? Please clarify:

1. Any maximum excerpt length, required attribution, logo/branding restrictions, and whether the original-posting link is required or sufficient.
2. Whether metadata and excerpts may be held transiently for matching and, if needed, cached; the allowed refresh/retention period and required deletion when a role closes or your permission ends.
3. Whether this permission covers the job-description text and any content supplied by your organization, and any third-party content we must exclude.
4. Whether you authorize use of the named public feed/API for this third-party educational display, or whether we need approval from the ATS provider or a partner program.
5. Any request limits, commercial-use restrictions, or other conditions.

We will use only documented public endpoints, avoid unlisted/private postings, apply source-specific request limits, and show no skill claim unless the excerpt directly supports it. We will not copy or store full descriptions. Please let us know whether written permission is available for this limited use and any conditions we should satisfy before testing.

Thank you,

Miva Buddy team
